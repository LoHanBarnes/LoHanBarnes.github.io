My summer break was quite fun! I went to a summer program at UCLA (ENG 1 Microcontrollers) and learned in depth about how microcontrollers work, the difference between an Arduino and a Raspberry Pi, and how to use them with different parts. At this program I also became good friends with Colby, one of our instructors, who is helping me a lot with my personal projects. During this class I also learned about the right laser cutter in the UCLA makerspace's issues with clear acrylic. Apparently you were supposed to cut it twice for that specific combination, because if you don't you will end up like me hunched over on the dusty floor trying to chisel out sections with a screw driver and a (half eaten?) mallet.
