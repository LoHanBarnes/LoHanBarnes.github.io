Chapter 1 of *To Engineer is Human* argues that engineers learn more from their failures than their successes. A design that works "breeds confidence" and may be weak in ways we can't see, and failures point out an issue we can solve and avoid repeating our mistakes. We study our failures such as in plane crashes or building collapses and work to improve and overcome the issues, and prevent it from occurring today.
