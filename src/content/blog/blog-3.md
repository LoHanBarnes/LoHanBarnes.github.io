The presentation I found the most interesting was the "Hyatt Regency Walkways in Kansas City (1978)" presentation. This event in which two suspended walkways inside the Hyatt Regency Hotel collapsed and caused the deaths of 114 people, was all due to one small design error of the hanger rod. This small change doubled the load on the walkway. I am fascinated by this tragedy more than the big ones such as Chernobyl since we can see the Hyatt Regency Walkway's engineering failure was a tiny and simple change, and yet had such a great impact on how it functioned. Because of this presentation I want to learn more about load and how to prevent replicating this mistake.
