---
title: "Blog #4: Education v. AI"
date: 2026-10-09
excerpt: "Where AI helps in learning, and where it doesn't."
---

I believe that AI can help in certain areas such as finding a certain object, formula, or mechanism, but should not be used by students or teachers to complete or assign study and learning material. None of my teachers use an excessive amount of AI, but many teachers create tests and homework walkthroughs with AI which is hard to understand since it sometimes covers topics the class hasn't touched upon, and the answers are sometimes incorrect which wastes a lot of my time debating if I or the study guide is correct. As a student, when teachers use AI to create an assignment or study guide I instantly feel like the product is lazy and dismiss the potential importance of it. Although logically I believe that if something is AI or not shouldn't matter if the information is true and communicated effectively, I would much rather have a human present the same information since usually it is easier to digest and their effort shows.
